# **App Name**: Diet Clinik Portal

## Core Features:

- Admin Login: Secure login for admin.
- Manage Subscription Plans: Add, edit, and delete subscription plans displayed on the public site.
- Add New Customers: Manually add new customers to the system.
- Create and Assign Diet Plans: Create diet plans and assign them to specific customers.
- View Customer Profiles: View each customer’s profile, including body measurements and tracking data.
- Track Diet Plan Compliance: Track daily diet plan compliance for each customer.
- Display Subscription Plans: Display subscription plans editable by admin.
- Payment Gateway Redirection: Redirect users to Razorpay payment gateway when they click on a plan.
- Post-Payment Redirection: Redirect users to the customer login/registration portal after payment confirmation.
- Customer Login and Profile Management: Secure login and profile management for customers.
- View and Renew Subscriptions: View current subscription details and renew subscriptions.
- Access Diet Plans: Access monthly assigned diet plans.
- View and Respond to Diet Plans: View diet plan details and reply with images & notes on food taken.
- Update Personal Details: Enter and update personal details including height, weight, body measurements, health problems, and allergies.
- Calculate BMI and BMR: Automatically calculate BMI & BMR from measurements.
- Log Measurements: Log daily/weekly weight & measurements.
- Generate Progress Graphs: Generate and display progress graphs for weight & body measurements.
- Receive Notifications and Reminders: Get real-time notifications and reminders.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft, natural green (#109a49) to evoke health and wellness.
- Background color: Light, desaturated beige (#F5F5DC).
- Accent color: Muted orange (#D2691E) for call-to-action buttons.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter' sans-serif font to give it a modern look
- Use simple, clean icons to represent different features and sections of the app.
- Maintain a clean, minimalist layout with clear information hierarchy for easy navigation.
- Incorporate subtle animations and transitions to enhance user experience.
